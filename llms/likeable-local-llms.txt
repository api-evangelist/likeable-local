# Likeable Local

> Likeable Local is a social media marketing company operating publicly as Storytellit. It sells
> white-label social media management to digital agencies — strategy, content creation and publishing,
> paid social advertising, lead capture, and monthly reporting. Both likeablelocal.com and
> www.likeablelocal.com redirect to storytellit.com; the login-gated product application at
> app.storytellit.com and app.likeablelocal.com still identifies itself as "Likeable Local".

## APIs

Likeable Local / Storytellit publishes no public API, developer portal, API documentation, or
machine-readable API specification as of 2026-07-19. There is no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL schema,
webhook catalog, SDK, CLI, or MCP server to reference.
